# Afterspan

Afterspan connects product work, its execution evidence, and the result a user needed.

## Language

**Goal**: A versioned definition of the result a product intends to achieve, including the population, deadline, required checks and evidence.

**Journey**: One user task across its attempts. A retry continues the task rather than creating a fresh success opportunity.

**Trace**: The observed execution of work, composed of timed steps. A trace explains execution; it does not itself prove a user outcome.

**Check**: A versioned evaluation of a stated criterion. Its result can pass, fail or remain unknown; its method and evidence must remain explicit.

**Delivery**: Acceptance or receipt of a result by a named channel. Delivery does not establish correctness, reading or usefulness.

**Outcome**: The observed result of a task under a declared goal. Completion, correctness and user value are distinct criteria.

**Evidence**: A scoped observation or receipt supporting a claim. Missing, conflicting or expired evidence cannot become a verified success.

**Experiment**: A comparison of candidate behaviors against the same versioned cases and checks. Passing examples alone do not establish a production improvement.

**Collection health**: Bounded evidence about declared sources, queue delivery, missing pulses and failures. Observed health does not prove that every task was instrumented.

**Source expectation**: Current editor configuration naming the source, environment, optional workflow and expected reporting interval. It is not a historical deployment inventory.
