# Afterspan build state

The requested outcome-platform release covers generic outcomes and evaluations, tracing SDKs and interoperability, failure-to-experiment-to-release workflows, and operational proof. The authoritative acceptance matrix is [Outcome platform release](docs/outcome-platform-release.md).

## Early production release

Primary Traces, nested timings and missing-evidence handling are deployed. Tracker's catalog, parameter and environment-comparison answers have pre-send source checks. Request environment and tool read scopes remain distinct. These checks are producer-side observations, not independent Afterspan source verification or full task usefulness.

Versioned task goals, source-evidence checks, Python/Node context, metadata-only OTLP, governed debugging, reviewed cases/datasets, application-side experiments, collection health and a read-only experiment-to-release outcome comparison are included. Existing release policies remain in force. Historical attestations and prior release evidence retain their original scope.

## In progress

The backend and Tracker are deployed. Two real read-only Tracker validation requests have bound pre-send source checks, provider-reported model charges and matching Slack acceptance. An isolated official exporter also passed against the production service. These do not establish complete answer fulfillment, normal-user improvement, second-product adoption or production scale. Remaining gates include streaming evidence, live experiment/release windows, scheduled recovery readiness and remaining scope/privacy checks; the acceptance matrix owns their evidence. Continuous evaluation, human annotation, alerting, prompt management and broad provider/framework integration remain product gaps.

## Other product work

Replay, surveys and optional hosted billing remain separate from this release. Existing documented billing verification limits still apply. No infrastructure scale, enterprise readiness or outcome improvement is claimed without its own measurements.
